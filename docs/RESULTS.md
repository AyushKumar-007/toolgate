## Baseline comparison

| Mode | Unauthorized pass ↓ | False block ↓ | Legit completion ↑ | Audit completeness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `direct` | 1.00 | 0.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| `prompt_only` | 1.00 | 0.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| `gateway` | **0.00** | **0.00** | **1.00** | 1.00 |

Interpretation:

- **direct** executes every proposal, so every attack decision is an unauthorized pass.
- **prompt_only** follows `case.prompt_only_would_execute`. Attack cases keep that flag true, so unauthorized pass remains 1.00. The baseline shows a prompt-side flag is not independent enforcement.
- **gateway** denies all eight attack decisions and completes all six legitimate tasks (false block 0.00).

